This Privacy Policy explains how Sarfees collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

## 1. Information we collect
- Account details: name, phone number, gender, language preference.
- Trip and package data: pickup and dropoff locations, times, fares, and statuses.
- Device data: app version, platform, push-notification token.

## 2. How we use your information
- To match you with drivers, deliver packages, and process trips.
- To send notifications about your trips, deliveries, and account.
- To improve service quality and detect fraud or abuse.

## 3. Data sharing
We share only the information necessary to fulfill your trip or delivery — for example, sharing a passenger name and pickup location with the assigned driver. We do not sell personal information to third parties.

## 4. Data retention
We retain trip and account data for as long as required by Jordanian law and for operational and dispute-resolution purposes.

## 5. Your rights
You may request deletion of your account at any time by contacting support@sarfees.com. Some records may be retained for legal compliance.

## 6. Security
We use industry-standard practices to protect your data, including encrypted transport (HTTPS) and hashed credentials.

## 7. Changes
We may update this policy. Material changes will be communicated through the app.
